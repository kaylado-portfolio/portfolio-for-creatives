Social Media Design

Second Snacks is a snack company who aims to help consumers achieve sustainable and ethical eating habits while on the go. Unlike others in the snack industry, our protein bars that utilize wasted coconut flesh and fruit rinds which would otherwise go to waste.

We aim to promote up-cycling and sustainable lifestyle changes that extend beyond buying a product. Through a friendly, inspiring and helpful social media presence, we strive to do more than just sell a snack. We want to show that up-cycling can be fun, creative and doesn’t have to feel like work.

Understanding and rethinking what we throw away can lead to better choices and a healthier planet.
