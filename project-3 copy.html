Overview

PLANETA POP is an electro music festival aimed to inspire curiosity and exploration of diverse cultures, connecting people through a love of electro pop. Featuring international artists from both the mainstream and the underground, the festival highlights cultural influences that evolve electronic music.

Challenge

In an era where we see much cultural divide, we don't see how music can connect us without being apart of the same culture.

Approach

We aim to inspire curiosity and exploration of diverse cultures and style, connecting people through a love of electro pop. PLANETA POP celebrates digital sound created and appreciated by everyone. You don’t need to understand a language to appreciate good music.

The Process

Parenting is a hard job, but we’re here to make it easy for you. We know how much time it takes to look for the tools and essentials needed for a cat, whether is be searching on the internet for the best of the best. We’re here to give you the simple and essential tools. Of course as you grow and learn with your cat you’ll discover their preferences, but for now we want to give you just a little easy push in the first direction.
