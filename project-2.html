Planeta Pop

Scope

  • Music Festival Concept

Role

  • Brand Identity /

Tools

  • Indesign / Photoshop / Illustrator / Figma

Overview

  • Music is a global art, but Western audiences  mostly listen in English. How might we invite them  to get curious about what they could be missing?
  • Planeta Pop is an electropop festival that celebrates  the world’s sounds. Through a diverse lineup of international artists, it invites audiences to discover music beyond their own language and connect through a shared love of the genre. Because you  don’t need to understand the lyrics to feel the beat.
